Street Feast — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

Street Feast (“the game”) is published by Shachar Sultan, an independent developer (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what information is handled when you play the game on iOS or Android, and the choices you have.

In short: I do not collect, store or sell any personal information. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to deliver them.

Information I collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. I do not receive your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

Your game progress

Your save (money, shops, cities, settings) is stored only on your device, in the app’s local storage. It is never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes your save, and you can also wipe it at any time from Settings → Reset everything inside the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded video ads — you choose when to watch one in exchange for an in-game bonus. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be inferred;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with the ads you see.

This information goes directly to Google; I do not receive it. Google’s handling of it is described in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the first time you open the game, Google’s consent form asks whether ads may be personalised. You can accept or decline.
  • iOS: the game does not ask for permission to track you across other apps, so ads on iOS are not personalised using your advertising identifier.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device’s Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).

Notifications

If you allow it, the game schedules reminders (for example, when your shops are full) using your device’s local notifications. They are created on your device; no push-notification service is used and nothing is sent to me.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact me and I will help.

Your rights

Because I hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. For data collected by Google through ads, you can use the controls above or Google’s tools at My Ad Center. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have additional rights; I do not sell or share personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
